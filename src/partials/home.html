Your first class

What to expect

New students

礼

Etiquette first

Practice begins with a bow to the shajo (shooting hall). You'll learn how to enter, where to stand, and how to handle the bow before you draw it.

型

Form before arrows

The shahō hassetsu, the eight stages of shooting, are practiced without an arrow at first. Wear loose clothing you can move in.

的

Then the target

Early shooting is at the makiwara, a straw target at arm's length. The 28-metre mato comes once the form is settled.
